Terms of Service

Daily Random Bot · Effective 13 October 2026

1. About the Bot

Daily Random Bot is a Discord bot that selects one eligible non-bot member of a participating server at random each day at 00:00 Bahrain time (UTC+3) and posts a mention with the message “You have been chosen today! 🎉” in a configured channel.

2. Use of the Bot

The bot is intended for servers where administrators have authorized its use. Server administrators are responsible for choosing an appropriate channel, informing members about daily mentions, and complying with Discord’s rules and applicable laws.

3. Random Selection

Every eligible non-bot member may be selected, including members selected on previous days. Selection does not guarantee fairness over a short period. The bot may not send a message if it is offline, lacks permissions, or cannot access the server or channel.

4. Availability and Changes

The bot is provided as-is without a guarantee of uninterrupted availability. Its operator may modify, suspend, or discontinue the bot at any time.

5. Abuse and Removal

Do not use the bot for harassment or unwanted repeated targeting. Server administrators may remove the bot from their server at any time.

6. Contact

For questions, concerns, or requests about this bot, contact the server administrator or bot operator through the Discord server where the bot is installed.

7. Updates

These terms may be updated when the bot’s functionality or practices change. Effective date: 13 October 2026.
